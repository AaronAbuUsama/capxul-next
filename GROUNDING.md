# Grounding: Aaron's strategy

This is the strategy stated by Aaron on 9 November 2026, restated for the current direction. The original is preserved at [archive/GROUNDING.md](archive/GROUNDING.md). For live scope and sequence use [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md), the plan and the blueprint's current rulings; this document does not override them.

## The honest starting point

The technology was not the problem: wallets, Safes, on-chain authority, Bridge and agents worked. The container was wrong. Screens designed in isolation did not provide the depth a real company expects from its business system. A company cannot move its business onto something missing basic payroll, HR, books, invoicing, CRM or projects; some also need stock and manufacturing.

The move is not to throw the technology away. **Put our technology inside a proven business system rather than rebuild the ERP class from scratch.**

## What we are building

**Capxul is an on-chain company:** one complete business system with Capxul's money and authority built in.

- **The business system** is built on ERPNext and Frappe: HR, payroll calculation, books, invoicing, CRM, projects and the other business functions companies need. We re-skin and extend it into one product, Capxul. Users and agents do not need to know the name Frappe.
- **Core is built in:** identity, organisation and hats, budgets, payments, governance and banking through Bridge accounts. The chain owns authority; business-system permissions reflect it. Safes and Bridge are the preferred banking path, not a prohibition on a company's other accounts.
- **Agents are first-class users**, operating through the same CLI/SDK door under their own authority. They earn their keep finding and qualifying leads, working pipelines, coordinating projects and doing routine HR administration. Agents nudge people about bills; people decide money.

We sell to small and mid-sized companies, around 15–25 people, including companies with no connection to crypto. They should see a business system, banking and permissions that work, not crypto mechanics they must learn.

**No country is assumed.** The first jurisdiction is open under `aaron.jurisdiction-open`, which supersedes both UK-first assumptions and the subsequent US-first ruling. A crypto- or blockchain-friendly place is preferred; UAE is a candidate, not a selection. Payroll must have real local depth—tax, statutory deductions, pensions and filings where applicable—but the jurisdiction and support route still need a decision.

## How we earn

- **Bridge banking:** the business system brings companies in; banking is central to the product and its revenue.
- **The hosted product:** companies pay for their company system.
- **A real forward-deployed engineer as a premium upsell:** a person learns the company and builds workflows and automations with agents. This is not the base product or a promise to run day-to-day operations. This strategic service is distinct from the hosted autonomous FDE feature, which is parked in the current blueprint.
- **Customers pay for the tokens their agents use**, helping fund the experiments that make the product better.

ERPNext's established market and partner ecosystem demonstrate that companies already pay for this class of software and its setup. We enter that market with a system, banking and agents, not a new category customers must first learn to want. Licensing remains a dependency to manage, not something to assume away.

## Dogfood first

**Launching means running our own Capxul company on Capxul:** our CRM, enrichment, agents and banking, actively finding and talking to customers. We are the first customer and feel every gap ourselves.

That validates whether customers will buy from us, produces real workflow and agent-run data, and creates a revenue-supported learning loop. Next comes an outside company, even initially free or on a deal; proof is a paying company, Bridge revenue and customers paying for agent tokens.

## The deeper bet

The system is also the harness for a drop-in worker: real companies on software agents can operate, environments in which to train and test those agents, and data and evals to improve them. Shared simulations with people and agents, and full agent simulations, should be judged by the same evals.

Each customer workflow becomes an environment; each setup leaves reusable templates; each agent run supplies evidence. Dogfooding, banking and the FDE service earn the revenue and data that make that long bet possible.

## Guardrails

- Do not rebuild an ERP from scratch. Use the existing depth and make it Capxul.
- Do not drop Core or banking: they are the trust layer and a revenue source.
- Do not sell “crypto” to companies that want a working business system.
- Do not lead with the FDE upsell or confuse it with the base product.
- Do not chase equity or other directions at the expense of our own operating company and paying customers. Equity is parked in current scope.
- Do not mistake strategic ambition for the next task. The plan sequences delivery; the blueprint records the design; checks and eval runs supply evidence.
