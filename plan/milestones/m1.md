## What it delivers

A book that describes the product we are actually building, at full detail, with nothing important left as a guess. Concretely: the old product's rebuilt Frappe features are archived; the joins between Core and Frappe (sign-in, the signer, roles following hats, books from the chain), company sites, feature switches, the door and worlds are written in; every operation says what it returns, which errors it gives, which events and spans it sends, and has an example; the machine and a production environment are records; and a check proves the book matches the contracts.

## Why it is first

Everything later is built from the book and tested against it. A book that describes the wrong product, or guesses, makes every later step wrong in the same direction.

## How we know it is done

The eight exit lines of the goal. Seven are about the book and can be checked by the build and the plan. The eighth is the working world, which needs milestones 2, 3 and the cloud.
