## What it delivers

The CLI as the one door to Frappe: any document, action or report, as the calling person. Every command recorded as one entry with who, which hats, the world's time, what it returned and what it changed. A world's clock that can be set and advanced, with the chain and every site following. Worlds saved and restored.

## Why it is here

This is the breadth. Once the door exists, agents can drive accounts, staff, projects and customers, and every run produces a record.

## How we know it is done

Agents take a lead to a paid invoice, onboard a staff member and close a month through the CLI alone, on a clock they moved, and the whole run can be read back from the record.
