## What it delivers

The test sites running on the real v4 contracts: a company Safe with authority installed, real hats, the real budget contract and the real payment contract with the document's hash. The stand-in contracts are deleted. A small service per world replaces the stub.

## Why it is here

The contracts are the hard-won part of Capxul and they already work. Putting them under the sites early shows what the Frappe side must carry (cost centre, account code) and whether any contract has to change.

## How we know it is done

The end-to-end script that passes today on the stand-in passes on the real contracts, with no network.

## Where it stands

The contracts, their 1,230 tests, the key signer and an 18-step proof are in the repository. They are not yet under the sites.
