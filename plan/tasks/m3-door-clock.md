## What
On the test sites: the recorder (one entry per command), the door (the CLI reaching any Frappe document, action or report as the calling person), the clock (set and advanced for a whole world, with the chain and every site following), and snapshots (a world saved, restored in place or as a separate world). It ends with agents taking a lead to a paid invoice, onboarding a staff member and closing a month.
## Why
It is the breadth, and it is the evidence for most of what the book currently marks as assumption. The biggest unknown is whether a Frappe site can follow a clock that is not the machine's.
## Done when
- The new checks in the end-to-end script pass.
- The findings say what follows the clock and what does not, how a person's session is obtained without a browser, and what a snapshot must rename.
