## What
Aaron reads the portal in the order listed on his Questions card (Overview, Questions, Concepts, Blueprint, Architecture, Worlds, Build, History, Docs), each stop with the one question it should answer, and says whether the book is fit to build from. His acceptance is of the build the portal names at its top ("Built from <commit>"); the coordinator records that commit with his answer.

## Why
It is the goal's last exit line. The checks can prove the book is consistent. Only he can say it is the right book. Without an order and a commit, the read has no end.

## Done when
- He accepts the book at a named commit, or says what is missing and that becomes tasks.
