## What
Aaron reads the portal, https://macbook.tail4b9a40.ts.net:8443, in this order, and says whether the book is fit to build from. His acceptance is of the build the portal names at its top ("Built from <commit>"); the coordinator records that commit with his answer. About an hour.

1. **Overview** (`/`). The climb: records, what waits for him, operations built, evals passing. Question: is this where you thought we were?
2. **Plan** (`/plan/`). The goal, its exit lines, the milestones and the critical path. Question: is this the right goal, and the right next work?
3. **Questions** (`/review/`). What waits on him. Question: is anything here not yours, or missing?
4. **Three concepts**: Payments (`/concept/payments/`), Organizations (`/concept/organizations/`) and Sites (`/concept/sites/`). Their operations, decisions and evals. Question: would a builder know exactly what to make from these pages?
5. **Two operations**: `payment.send` (`/operation/payment.send/`) and `org.create` (`/operation/org.create/`). Steps, refusals, examples, telemetry and their evals. Question: is each claim one you would hold the product to?
6. **Architecture** (`/design/`). The components and how they talk, Frappe-native. Question: is this the shape you ruled for?
7. **Worlds** (`/worlds/`). The worlds and the clock. Question: is this how you want to test time and money?
8. **Build** (`/build/`). What exists in code today and what is proven. Question: does it say only what is true?
9. **Docs** (`/docs/`). The written decisions and direction. Question: does anything contradict what you have said since?

## Why
It is the goal's last exit line. The checks can prove the book is consistent. Only he can say it is the right book. Without an order and a commit, the read has no end.
