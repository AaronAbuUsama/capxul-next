## What
A template world saved once; an adapter that replays an old company's facts through the new door; the scenario manifest (seats, script, clock plan, scoring, end) and the runner that plays its script. First scenario: one company for three months, then the town of four.
## Why
A world is only useful with companies and events in it. The old scenario companies are the source.
## Done when
- run create from a scenario gives a world that plays its script on a stepped clock, twice, with the same result
