## What
Record the decision on how cheaply a world runs: one small service per world with one database file, the indexer folded into the service, no bundler in simulated worlds, idle worlds parked and restored on demand, and the real chain kept per world. Moving the Capxul part onto Cloudflare is recorded as a later option, not taken now.
## Why
The cost research showed the chain is tiny and the plumbing around it is most of a world's cost. The service has to be designed lean from the start.
## Done when
- The decision is in the book with the measured and estimated figures and what would prove it faithful.
