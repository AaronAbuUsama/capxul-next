## What
Cohort and run commands; assigning seats to agents; watching runs; closing; exporting one dataset per run with its manifest and lineage; the quality checks that accept or reject it (a gap-free sequence, the books agreeing with the chain, no secret, redaction of personal data).
## Why
The product of a run is clean data. A hundred or two hundred testers and agents need runs that are scheduled, watched, closed and checked.
## Done when
- Ten agent runs of one scenario are scheduled with one command and each ends with an accepted dataset or a stated reason
