## What
Design in Core, then build: a pop-up that shows what is being signed, lets the person sign the transaction, and authenticates them. It replaces the stand-in sign-in on the test sites, which lets anyone become any test person. Until it exists, worlds are specified and built for agents, and human seats and viewer links stay closed.
## Why
Aaron: this belongs to Capxul Core and is on the critical path, though not yet. Agents hold keys and sign with them. People need a way to prove who they are and to approve a transaction, and no person should use a world's screens before it exists.
## Done when
- A person signs in to a company's site through Capxul and approves a payment in the pop-up, with no stand-in involved
- The stand-in sign-in is removed from every world a person can reach
