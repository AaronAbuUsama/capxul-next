## What
One coherent design in the book and on the portal: a world as a hosted thing with an address; a core profile with no sites and a full profile with sites on the one shared bench; connecting the CLI to a world; identity that travels with a key, so a person with keys joins a new world in one command; scenarios; runs and cohorts; the record as a dataset with its manifest, lineage, reproducibility and quality checks; the reset, step, observation and reward interface for learning; viewer access; staging and production as kinds of world. Written as operations, journeys and decisions, plus one readable document and a Worlds page.
## Why
Aaron: this is a core, foundational part and must be specified well before breadth. Heavy infrastructure on every sandbox makes no sense; it must be easy to create a simulation, bring a hundred or two hundred testers and agents through scenarios, and get clean training data and environments for reinforcement learning. Today the pieces are scattered across five documents and a few dozen records.
## Done when
- The portal has a Worlds page that explains the whole design to a person who has read nothing else
- Every command needed to create a scenario run, join it, close it and export its data is an operation in the book with an example
- The book says what each profile costs and how many run at once, with measured figures marked
