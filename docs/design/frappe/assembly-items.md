# Frappe redesign: what the lanes asked of other concepts

Collected from the four lanes' reports (accounting, payroll, CRM, projects and start-up), for the assembly pass.

- **From accounting:** payments: - Pass with each money side to books.record-money-fact: safe_address, log_index, the counterparty's directory party_id, and fee_raw where known. - Pass the `against` the command carried: payment send's --account and --suggest, a party, a request's meaning, a payout's account, or a head's 'Pay with Capxul' references. Today nothing carries --account to books. - The payment command must store that choice from its birth, so it reaches books at settlement.
- **From accounting:** banking: - Pass fee_raw for a payout (the quote's fee_amount) and for a deposit (gross less net, in token units). - Put the payout's account and payee party into the command it prepares through payments.prepare-command. - Pass log_index and party_id on the deposit's and the return's book sides.
- **From accounting:** requests: - A paid request's side should carry its meaning to books: its income account, and, when a head made a Sales Invoice for it, party Customer and that invoice as the reference. - Mapping a Capxul request to the head's Sales Invoice is a requests or head design.
- **From accounting:** payroll: - A collection reaches books as an unclassified payment out, because payroll cannot invoke books and payments does not know agreements. To pre-classify collections, the account must come through payments' birth of the collection, or from the head, which makes the contractor's Purchase Invoice or the Employee's posting and annotates the row. - Frappe HR net pay against a Payroll Entry posts as a Bank Entry on Payroll Payable (2200, role payroll_payable), with party Employee and reference Payroll Entry. Withheld deductions are paid later against their components' liability accounts.
- **From accounting:** organizations (start-up and the head connection): - The start-up or FDE step should run `org books accounts import` for a new Organization. Books makes no chart by itself, so the frozen 'no chart until one is made' world stays reachable. - default_currency must be USD or a currency whose USD Chainlink pair the deployment lists, or the import refuses it. - A time zone or fiscal year start changed after the import does not move the books. - The head connection should: connect only charted books; give the head's agent a Seat wearing the Books hat; apply Capxul's chart through the Chart of Accounts Importer before any transaction; map ERPNext's auto-made root cost centre and Main onto Capxul's (capxul_cost_center); set the Company's default accounts to the role accounts; make the Fiscal Years; and set Accounts Settings enable_immutable_ledger, the repost-allowed types, frozen Safe accounts and a Currency per token.
- **From accounting:** parties and contacts (CRM): a voucher's party is a directory party_id. The head's app finds or creates the Customer, Supplier or Employee by the Custom Field capxul_party, the CRM lane's name. A head GL line whose party has no Party is accepted with a null party_id.
- **From accounting:** projects: an annotation's `project` is opaque text today. Add concept_projects to books' dependsOn in shared/foundation.ts (and dependencies.of-books) so books.annotations.save can check it against projects' table. Projects already sits below books.
- **From accounting:** shared: - foundation.ts: concept_books.about should name the Company, the Account and Cost Center trees, the gap-free feed and the head's vouchers. - The deployment's asset configuration (books.capxul-assets) gains each Company currency's Chainlink USD pair; books.prices.sync reads them. - journeys.ts may extend close-the-books with a head following books.sync.feed.
- **From payroll:** Shared foundation (shared/foundation.ts): add concept_payments to concept_payroll.dependsOn. Rewrite concept_payroll.about: Frappe HR's payroll (Employees, structures, assignments, runs, slips) beside the v4 agreements, with runs paid through payments.prepare-command.
- **From payroll:** Shared decisions (shared/decisions.ts): add payments to dependencies.of-payroll. Rewrite dependencies.payments-payroll: payroll depends on payments for a run's payment and its settlement; payments still never depends on payroll. Mark concepts.payroll superseded by payroll.scope.
- **From payroll:** Shared landings (shared/landings.ts OLD_AREAS): payroll/runs now lands in concept_payroll by payroll.scope, note "Frappe HR's Payroll Entry and Salary Slip". payroll/groups and payroll/terms stay dropped.
- **From payroll:** Builder, once the payroll→payments edge exists: add `import * as staged from "./staged.ts"` and `staged` to books/target/payroll/index.ts modules. A scratch build with the edge and staged registered shows 0 problems and closes the 10 payroll undriven-transition gaps.
- **From payroll:** Payments: payments.prepare-command takes purpose "payroll". PreparedItem takes an optional source { kind: "salary-slip" | "payroll-remittance", id, payroll_entry_id, employee_id }, kept on the Payment and its book fact, and an optional payee_mailbox for a payee with no account.
- **From payroll:** Payments: rewrite payments.claim-escrow-routes to aaron.budget-pay-unclaimed. A budget-route item to a mailbox with no live Safe becomes a claim escrow funded by the budget. Its refund goes back to the budget, and the budget's holder or the Board may take it back. payment_transitioned moves it to escrowed, to settled on the claim, and back with outcome refunded.
- **From payroll:** Payments: document that payments.settled_at is the settling block's time, and keep settled_tx_hash and settled_block_number readable by the payer's tenant (C6). Payroll's staged run process copies them onto the slip.
- **From payroll:** Books (accounting lane), the accrual: post a submitted run's accrual (Frappe's make_accrual_jv_entry) through a door payroll's runs.submit can invoke, keyed by the Payroll Entry, and reverse it on runs.cancel. Books cannot depend on payroll; that would be a cycle through payments. The lines: debit each earning's account; credit each deduction's liability_account; credit Payroll Payable per net with party Employee; employer contributions debit expense and credit liability.
- **From payroll:** Books, the Bank Entry: post one Bank Entry per settled payroll Payment. Debit Payroll Payable (party Employee, reference_type Payroll Entry), credit the treasury account of the company Safe and token; cheque_no is the tx, cheque_date and posting_date the block's date. Remittance items debit the component's liability.
- **From payroll:** Books, escrow and collections: post escrow clearing entries (into a Capxul Escrow account, then to Payroll Payable on the claim, then back to treasury on a refund). Post an agreement collection as expense with party Employee, or a Supplier Payment Entry for an Organization payee. Provide the default Payroll Payable, salary expense and treasury accounts that payroll stores as books' account ids.
- **From payroll:** Frappe head lane, Frappe-side setup with no fork: the Capxul app's Custom Fields (capxul_id on all six doctypes; capxul_party_id on Employee; capxul_payee_party_id on Salary Component; capxul_budget_id and capxul_agreement_id on SSA; capxul_budget_id and capxul_command_id on Payroll Entry; capxul_status, capxul_payment_id, capxul_chain_payment_id, capxul_tx_hash, capxul_paid_at, capxul_document_hash and capxul_computed_by on Salary Slip). Property Setters make Employee gender and date_of_birth optional. Set System Settings rounding_method to "Banker's Rounding"; Payroll Settings include_holidays_in_total_working_days=1, disable_rounded_total=1 and email_salary_slip_to_employee=0. Hide Payroll Entry's Make Bank Entry with a Client Script.

Pull payroll's six tables through a gap-free change feed (activity's, or one for every Frappe-shaped concept). Check a Capxul-made run by Frappe's recompute before submitting the head's copy. Show collection earnings as Additional Salary. Never take the pay mailbox from the head.
