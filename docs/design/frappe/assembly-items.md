# Frappe redesign: what the lanes asked of other concepts

Collected from the four lanes' reports (accounting, payroll, CRM, projects and start-up), for the assembly pass. Each item ends with its status after the assembly (groups edges, rules, money, ledger, identity and world, then the build), with the records that carry it out. "Deferred" names the pass or owner that takes it.

- **From accounting:** payments: - Pass with each money side to books.record-money-fact: safe_address, log_index, the counterparty's directory party_id, and fee_raw where known. - Pass the `against` the command carried: payment send's --account and --suggest, a party, a request's meaning, a payout's account, or a head's 'Pay with Capxul' references. Today nothing carries --account to books. - The payment command must store that choice from its birth, so it reaches books at settlement.
  **Status: done.** Each side carries its Safe, log index, block time, counterparty Party, `fee_raw` and `against`, kept on the Payment from birth; `payment send --account` and `--suggest` reach books, a person's through an annotate step (`payments.money-sides`, `payments.lifecycle.payment`, `payments.commands.prepare`, `client.payments.pay`).
- **From accounting:** banking: - Pass fee_raw for a payout (the quote's fee_amount) and for a deposit (gross less net, in token units). - Put the payout's account and payee party into the command it prepares through payments.prepare-command. - Pass log_index and party_id on the deposit's and the return's book sides.
  **Status: done.** `fee_raw`, the payee's Party as Supplier and a non-zero document hash go through the door; the deposit and return sides carry Safe, log index and Party (`banking.payout-books`, `banking.payout-document-hash`, `banking.payouts.quote`). A party on a line whose account cannot carry one stays off the voucher (`books.party-lines`).
- **From accounting:** requests: - A paid request's side should carry its meaning to books: its income account, and, when a head made a Sales Invoice for it, party Customer and that invoice as the reference. - Mapping a Capxul request to the head's Sales Invoice is a requests or head design.
  **Status: done.** A request keeps its income account and the head's Sales Invoice, and `inbox pay` passes them as `payee_against` (`requests.income-meaning`). Mapping to the head's Sales Invoice is the head's (same decision). A matched bank deposit waits in review (`banking.matched-deposit-meaning`). **Deferred:** an outside payer's invoice-escrow payout carries no meaning yet; the escrow redesign (task 65, `commands-and-escrow`) owns that path.
- **From accounting:** payroll: - A collection reaches books as an unclassified payment out, because payroll cannot invoke books and payments does not know agreements. To pre-classify collections, the account must come through payments' birth of the collection, or from the head, which makes the contractor's Purchase Invoice or the Employee's posting and annotates the row. - Frappe HR net pay against a Payroll Entry posts as a Bank Entry on Payroll Payable (2200, role payroll_payable), with party Employee and reference Payroll Entry. Withheld deductions are paid later against their components' liability accounts.
  **Status: done.** A person's paid collection accrues through the books door and posts on Payroll Payable with party Employee; an Organization payee's waits in review or for the head's Purchase Invoice (`payroll.collection-accrual`, `books.payroll-postings`). Net pay posts one Bank Entry per slip Payment on Payroll Payable with reference Payroll Entry (`books.payroll-postings`, `payroll.employee-wise-accounting`). Remittances are ordinary budget payments on the component's liability (`payroll.deduction-remittance`).
- **From accounting:** organizations (start-up and the head connection): - The start-up or FDE step should run `org books accounts import` for a new Organization. Books makes no chart by itself, so the frozen 'no chart until one is made' world stays reachable. - default_currency must be USD or a currency whose USD Chainlink pair the deployment lists, or the import refuses it. - A time zone or fiscal year start changed after the import does not move the books. - The head connection should: connect only charted books; give the head's agent a Seat wearing the Books hat; apply Capxul's chart through the Chart of Accounts Importer before any transaction; map ERPNext's auto-made root cost centre and Main onto Capxul's (capxul_cost_center); set the Company's default accounts to the role accounts; make the Fiscal Years; and set Accounts Settings enable_immutable_ledger, the repost-allowed types, frozen Safe accounts and a Currency per token.
  **Status: done, two parts declined.** Start-up: the chart is `org books accounts import`, run by the FDE or a Books-hat wearer in the first sitting (`books.startup`, `organizations.startup`). An unpriced currency is refused (`books.accounts.import`, `books.capxul-assets`). The Company's settings are fixed at the chart (`books.company-settings`). Head connection: the app adopts Main, sets the defaults, Fiscal Years, a Currency per token, frozen Safe accounts and the immutable ledger before the first push (`books.head-setup`, this pass). **Declined:** connecting only charted books (a head connects any time and books' push waits for the chart), the head's agent on a Seat with the Books hat (`books.sync`: Capxul calls, the head only rings), and the Chart of Accounts Importer (`books.head-setup`: it needs an empty Company).
